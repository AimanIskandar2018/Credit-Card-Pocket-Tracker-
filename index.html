Which card should I use?

🚗 ⛽ 🛒 🍽️ UOB for these four

UOB can give 10% cashback, up to RM15 per category, if you reach RM1,500 total eligible spend for its billing cycle.

🌤 Weekend? Maybank Amex

There is no minimum spend: each eligible weekend purchase earns 5%, until you reach RM50 cashback. RM1,000 of eligible weekend spending usually maximises it.

📆 Weekdays on Amex

Amex earns up to 5× TreatsPoints instead of the weekend cashback.

🍽 Hong Leong Wise

RM1,000 posted spend per calendar month unlocks up to 15% weekend dining, 10% weekend groceries/petrol and 1% selected online, subject to caps.

Use whichever card gives the best eligible rewards. Never spend extra just to hit a target.
